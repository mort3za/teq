Find unwanted accounts on X (Twitter) by their name, then block them safely. 🚫

Use it for spam, bots, scams, hate, a topic you do not want to see, or any other reason.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Write some words, emoji or phrases.
2. Scroll X as usual. Teq collects the accounts that match.
3. Look at the list and pick the accounts to block.
4. Teq blocks them for you, slowly, so X does not think you are a bot.

Nothing is blocked until you say so. Teq never blocks your own account.

🔒 PRIVATE
No sign-up, no server, no ads, no tracking. Your rules and lists stay in your browser.
